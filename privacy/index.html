Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Roundhouse Workout Timer ("Roundhouse", "the app") is published by Greg Fiest ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Roundhouse has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not receive any information about you or your workouts.

What we collect

Nothing. Roundhouse does not send personal information, usage data, workout data or device identifiers to us or to any advertising or analytics company. We do not use cookies, tracking SDKs or third-party analytics.

What stays on your Roku device

To work, the app saves a small amount of data in your Roku device's storage for this app only:

  • Workouts you build and save as favorites (names, exercises, rounds and times you enter)
  • Settings, such as whether audio cues are on
  • That you have read the health and safety notice
  • The progress of a workout in progress, so it can resume after an interruption

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by removing Roundhouse from your Roku home screen.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising, because we do not collect it.

Talk to a doctor first

Consult a physician or other qualified health professional before starting any exercise program or changing your current one. This matters most if you are pregnant or postpartum, are over 40 and new to exercise, have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, joint problems or any other medical condition, are taking medication, are recovering from an injury or surgery, or have been inactive.

Stop if something feels wrong

Stop exercising right away and seek medical help if you feel chest pain or pressure, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, an irregular heartbeat, or sharp or worsening pain. High-intensity formats such as Tabata, EMOM and AMRAP are demanding; go at a pace that is safe for you and rest whenever you need to, regardless of what the timer shows.

Your space and equipment

  • Clear enough room to move freely, away from the TV, furniture, stairs and sharp edges.
  • Make sure the TV is securely mounted or anchored so it cannot be knocked over.
  • Keep children and pets clear of the space while you work out.
  • Use any weights or equipment as their maker intends, and check them for damage before use.
  • Wear suitable footwear, work out on a stable, non-slip surface, and stay hydrated.

Sample workouts and the timer

Sample workouts are general examples, not a program designed for you. Adjust or skip anything that is not right for you. The timer is intended for general fitness use and may not be exact; do not rely on it for medical, rehabilitation or other safety-critical timing.
